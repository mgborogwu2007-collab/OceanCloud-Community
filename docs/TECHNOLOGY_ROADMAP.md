OceanCloud — Technology and Engineering Roadmap

Our Vision

OceanCloud is an ambitious, modular technology ecosystem intended to develop software products, cloud services, internet technologies, developer tools, communication platforms and digital infrastructure.

We welcome developers, engineers, designers, researchers, security professionals and other qualified contributors who are interested in helping us develop this vision.

This roadmap describes our intended areas of engineering. It does not mean every listed product or technology has already been built or is ready for public use.

Engineering Principles

OceanCloud aims to follow these principles:

1. Modular and maintainable architecture.
2. Security and privacy by design.
3. Reliable testing and code review.
4. Clear technical documentation.
5. Expandable systems without arbitrary product or category limits.
6. Responsible and lawful technology development.
7. Transparent contributor expectations.
8. Careful evaluation of external contributions.
9. Sustainable development and realistic project planning.

Project Status and Expectations

OceanCloud is an evolving project. Some areas are under development, some are planned, and others are long-term research goals.

Participation is currently voluntary and unpaid. There is no guaranteed salary, future compensation or equity. Any future compensation or ownership arrangement would require a separate, mutually agreed written agreement.

Contributors should begin with clearly scoped tasks and follow the project's contribution, security and review procedures.

We value responsible engineering, transparency, teamwork and sustainable progress.
