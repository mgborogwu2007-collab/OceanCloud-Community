Welcome to OceanCloud

OceanCloud is an independent technology project with an ambitious long-term vision: to develop an integrated technology ecosystem connecting digital services, businesses, developers and internet infrastructure.

We are building OceanCloud with a modular architecture designed to support multiple products and services under one technology ecosystem.

Our ambition is to develop useful, reliable, secure and scalable technology.

🌍 Our Vision

We envision OceanCloud growing into a technology ecosystem spanning applications, cloud infrastructure, networking, digital communication, developer services and eventually broader connectivity infrastructure.

Our long-term vision includes:

- Cloud computing and infrastructure.
- Web hosting and digital storage.
- DNS, CDN and network services.
- Domain registration and management.
- Business and transactional email.
- Defensive cybersecurity services.
- Developer platforms and collaboration tools.
- Social networking and communication.
- Web browser technology.
- Digital mapping and location services.
- Advertising and business technology.
- Future network and connectivity infrastructure.

These are development ambitions, not claims that all these services are currently operational.

💼 Collaboration and Financial Transparency

OceanCloud is currently an early-stage, self-funded project.

At this stage, we do not have the financial resources to offer salaries or guaranteed payments.

Participation is voluntary. Please apply only if you freely agree to contribute under these conditions.

The founder hopes to establish a formal OceanCloud company in the future and may explore equity opportunities for eligible long-term contributors.

No salary, shares, ownership percentage or future payment is guaranteed. Any future equity arrangement would require separate negotiations, appropriate legal documentation and mutual agreement.

🔐 Privacy, Security and Project Ownership

OceanCloud's main engineering repository remains private.

Our collaboration principles include:

- Controlled access to engineering resources.
- Review of all submitted code.
- Clear contributor responsibilities.
- Protection of confidential information.
- Written agreements covering intellectual property and confidentiality before substantial proprietary contributions.
- Respectful and professional communication.

Public participation in this community does not grant access to private OceanCloud source code.

🚀 How to Become a Contributor

If you are interested in joining the OceanCloud contributor community, please prepare the following information:

1. Your name or professional username.
2. Your country and time zone.
3. Your technical skills.
4. Your GitHub profile.
5. Examples of your previous projects.
6. The OceanCloud division you would like to contribute to.
7. Why you are interested in OceanCloud.

Applications will be reviewed individually.

Initial collaboration may begin with a small, clearly defined trial assignment.

📢 Contact and Community

Our initial recruitment and public communication will take place through GitHub.

Private WhatsApp communication may be offered to applicants after screening.

Please do not publish private phone numbers, passwords, access tokens or confidential information in public discussions.
