
# OceanCloud Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

OceanCloud welcomes people from every country, background and level of technical experience.

We are committed to creating a respectful, professional, safe and collaborative environment for everyone participating in the OceanCloud community.

## Expected behaviour

All community members are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and professionalism.
- Communicate honestly and constructively.
- Welcome different technical opinions and experiences.
- Give useful feedback without insulting or humiliating others.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Respect the time and voluntary contributions of others.
- Acknowledge other people's work and contributions.
- Follow project rules and applicable laws.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, bullying or intimidation.
- Discrimination or hateful conduct.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Publishing passwords, API keys or confidential source code.
- Impersonation, fraud or deliberate misrepresentation.
- Deliberately disrupting project activities.
- Claiming another person's work as your own.
- Unauthorised access to accounts, systems or repositories.

## Reporting problems

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the OceanCloud project leadership privately through the project's GitHub contact channels.

Do not publish sensitive personal information or security credentials in a public issue or discussion.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Depending on the circumstances, project leadership may:

1. Issue a warning.
2. Request corrective action.
3. Temporarily restrict participation.
4. Remove content that violates community rules.
5. Revoke repository access.
6. End a person's participation in the community.

Serious security, privacy or safety violations may result in immediate access restrictions.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to OceanCloud Community discussions, issues, pull requests, project communication channels and other spaces officially operated by OceanCloud.

## Final statement

We believe good technology is built through respect, responsibility, honesty and teamwork.

Thank you for helping make OceanCloud a welcoming and professional community.
